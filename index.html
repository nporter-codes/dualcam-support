如何拍摄

  1. 从首页或中间的拍摄入口进入，允许使用相机和麦克风。实际双摄录制需要支持 MultiCam 的 iPhone。
  2. 拖动前摄小窗调整位置，双击小窗选择形状与大小。确认后开始录制，录制期间布局固定。
  3. 默认关闭“保存前后原片”：录完直接得到一条前后画中画视频，不需要再做后期合成。
  4. 录制前开启“保存前后原片”：只保存前摄、后摄两条独立视频。你可以直接使用，也可以在之后选择本机重合成。

常见问题

拍摄画面黑屏或无法录制

在系统设置中允许 DualCam 使用相机与麦克风，确认镜头未被遮挡、设备支持前后同时采集，并检查可用存储空间。发生相机中断或设备过热时，请按 App 提示恢复后重试。不要删除尚未保存的作品来排障。

如何调整小窗与重新合成？

开拍前双击前摄小窗调整形状和小／中／大三档大小，拖动可改位置。只有保留了两路完整原片的作品才提供本机重合成；新视频不会覆盖原片。原片指编码视频文件，并非传感器 RAW。

水印与免费额度如何计算？

本机前三个不同导出作品无水印，此后仍可免费导出带 DualCam 水印的视频。同一作品重复导出不重复计数；一组前后原片计一个作品，新合成视频单独计数。失败或取消不扣次数。拍摄、保存原片和本机合成本身可免费使用。

如何恢复购买或取消订阅？

在“我的”打开 DualCam PRO，点击“恢复购买”。订阅请在 iPhone“设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅”管理，也可使用 Apple 订阅管理。删除 App 不会取消订阅。永久解锁不会自动取消已有订阅；月／年试用资格以 Apple 确认页为准。

视频和存储如何管理？

在 App 的本地作品／存储页面管理视频。保存到系统相册时仅请求添加权限，不读取整个相册。删除 App 内作品不会删除已保存的相册副本；删除 App 可能移除仍仅在其容器中的视频，请先导出重要作品。

反馈问题时需要提供什么？

提供 iPhone 型号、iOS 与 App 版本、操作步骤、原片开关状态及错误提示即可。请勿在公开工单上传个人影像或购买收据。退款申请请通过 Apple 官方渠道处理。

Start recording

  1. Open Capture from Home or the center tab and allow camera and microphone access. Real dual capture requires a compatible MultiCam iPhone.
  2. Drag the selfie window or double-tap it to select its shape and size. Confirm before recording; the layout stays fixed during capture.
  3. Leave Save Originals off for one picture-in-picture video directly after recording and file finalization.
  4. Turn Save Originals on before recording to keep only two separate front/back clips. Use them independently or compose a new video locally afterward.

Frequently asked questions

Black preview or recording unavailable

Check camera/microphone permission in iOS Settings, uncovered lenses, device compatibility and free storage. Follow the app’s instructions after camera interruptions or overheating. Keep unsaved recordings while troubleshooting.

Selfie window and recomposition

Before recording, double-tap the window for its shape and small/medium/large sizes, or drag to reposition it. Local recomposition requires both original clips. It produces a new file without overwriting originals, which are encoded videos rather than sensor RAW.

Free exports and PRO

The first three distinct exported works on this device are watermark-free. Later exports remain free with a watermark. Re-exporting a work does not count again. An original pair counts as one work; each new composition counts separately. Failed/canceled exports do not count. Capture, originals and local composition are free; PRO unlocks unlimited watermark-free exports.

Restore, cancel or request a refund

Open Me → DualCam PRO → Restore Purchases. Manage subscriptions in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions or Apple subscription management. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Lifetime does not cancel existing subscriptions. Trial eligibility is determined by Apple. Request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Local files and Photos

Manage recordings in the local library/storage screen. Saving to Photos uses add-only access. Removing an in-app work does not delete copies already saved to Photos. Deleting the app may remove videos still inside its container; export important works first.

Report a problem

Include your iPhone model, iOS/app version, steps, Originals mode and the error message. Do not upload private footage or receipts to public issues.
