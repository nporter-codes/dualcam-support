隐私政策

你的视频，留在你的手机。

Read in English ↓ · 生效／更新日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日

本政策适用于 DualCam iOS 应用。网站支持入口见支持中心，隐私相关问题可通过开发者支持工单提出。工单公开可见，请勿公开个人影像、密码或支付资料。

1. 拍摄内容与本机处理

相机画面、麦克风声音、合成视频及可选的两路原片由 iOS 在设备上采集、保存和处理。DualCam 没有开发者运营的视频上传服务器，也不要求创建 DualCam 账号。开发者不接收你的拍摄影像或声音。

2. 请求的权限

  • 相机与麦克风：用于双摄画面和现场声音。不授予相应权限将无法进行带声音的双摄录制。
  • 相册添加：在你保存视频时请求，用于写入相册；应用不读取整个系统照片库。

你可以在 iOS 设置中修改权限。向其他应用分享视频由你主动发起，接收方按其自己的隐私政策处理。

3. 本机设置、额度与诊断

应用在本机保存欢迎／引导状态、小窗布局、作品清单、合成估时等设置。前三个无水印作品的额度记录在本机 Keychain 中，以作品标识的哈希计数，不含拍摄内容。它不用于广告、跨应用跟踪或向开发者发送标识；部分 Keychain 记录可能在重新安装后保留。

本机有限长度的诊断日志用于排查相机、编码和文件故障，可能包含事件时间、设备／画质状态和错误信息，不包含相机图像或麦克风采样，应用不会自动上传这些日志。文件时间戳用于本机文件完整性检查，可用空间用于避免存储不足，系统时钟用于应用内计时／动画。

4. 购买与第三方服务

购买、付款、订阅及试用由 Apple 通过 App Store／StoreKit 处理。应用读取 Apple 提供的商品价格和已验证交易／权益状态以提供 PRO。开发者不获取银行卡信息，也不要求你向开发者提供 Apple 账户密码。Apple 按其隐私政策处理相关信息。

当前版本不集成第三方广告或分析 SDK，不进行广告跟踪，也不出售用户数据。访问本网站时，GitHub Pages 可能处理提供托管服务所需的网络信息，例如 IP 地址；请参阅 GitHub 隐私声明。本网站不添加分析脚本、表单或追踪 Cookie。

5. 保留、删除与控制

录制文件在你删除之前保存在本机。你可以在应用内删除原片或作品；系统相册及你主动分享的副本需在对应应用中另行管理。删除应用可能移除应用容器中的视频，但不会自动取消订阅或删除已导出的副本。撤回权限不会自动删除既有作品。应用没有服务器账号，因此没有服务器账号注销流程。

6. 儿童与政策更新

DualCam 是通用拍摄工具，不提供面向儿童的账号或社交服务。当前政策与应用的实际处理行为一致；如未来增加数据处理或服务，将更新政策并在需要时请求权限。此页的日期会随实质更新调整。

Privacy Policy

Your videos stay on your device.

Effective and updated November 18, 2026.

This policy covers the DualCam iOS app. For privacy questions, use the developer support issue linked from our support page. Issues are public; do not post private footage, passwords or payment information.

1. Recording and on-device processing

Camera images, microphone audio, composed videos and optional separate clips are captured, stored and processed on your device using iOS. DualCam has no developer-operated video upload server and requires no DualCam account. The developer does not receive your recorded footage or audio.

2. Permissions

  • Camera and microphone: used for dual-camera video and scene audio. They are necessary for dual recording with sound.
  • Add to Photos: requested when you save a video. The app does not read your entire system photo library.

Control permissions in iOS Settings. Sharing to another app is initiated by you, and that recipient handles the content under its own privacy policy.

3. Local settings, allowance and diagnostics

The app stores onboarding state, window layouts, recording manifests and composition timing estimates locally. The first-three-clean-works allowance is recorded in the device Keychain using hashed work identifiers, without footage. It is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking, or transmitted to the developer. Some Keychain records may remain after reinstalling.

Bounded local diagnostic logs help investigate camera, encoding and file errors. They may include event times, device/quality state and errors, but no camera images or microphone samples. The app does not automatically upload these logs. File timestamps support local integrity checks, disk capacity checks prevent storage failures, and the system clock supports in-app timing and animation.

4. Purchases and other services

Apple handles purchases, payments, subscriptions and trials through the App Store and StoreKit. The app reads product prices and verified transaction/entitlement information from Apple to enable PRO. The developer receives no card details and never asks for your Apple Account password. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

The current version contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDK and performs no advertising tracking or sale of user data. GitHub Pages may process network information such as your IP address to host this website; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website adds no analytics scripts, forms or tracking cookies.

5. Retention and control

Recordings remain on your device until you remove them. Delete clips or works inside the app; manage copies in Photos and recipient apps separately. Deleting DualCam may remove videos in its container, but does not cancel subscriptions or delete exported copies. Withdrawing permission does not erase existing recordings. There is no server account to delete.

6. Children and updates

DualCam is a general camera tool with no child account or social service. This policy describes the current app. If future services change data handling, we will update it and request permissions where needed. The date above reflects substantive updates.
